# Runbook: Electrical Incidents

## Immediate actions (today)

- Stop work and secure the affected equipment or area.
- Do **not** touch exposed conductors or injured persons until power is isolated.
- Isolate power using lockout/tagout (LOTO) where possible.
- Provide medical assistance and call emergency services if shock or burns occurred.
- Notify the responsible electrician or maintenance lead.

## Short-term (next 7 days)

- Review LOTO records and confirm procedures were followed.
- Inspect equipment for damage, overheating, or improper modifications.
- Verify that only qualified personnel have access to electrical panels.
- Check availability and visibility of warning labels and signage.

## Preventive (next 30 days)

- Audit other similar installations for the same failure mode.
- Refresh training on electrical safety, LOTO, and use of test equipment.
- Update maintenance schedules and inspection checklists if needed.
- Document the incident and actions taken in the safety system.
