# Runbook: PPE Noncompliance

## Immediate actions (today)

- Stop the unsafe activity if there is imminent risk.
- Provide required PPE (e.g. hard hat, safety glasses, gloves, high-visibility vest).
- Explain the specific hazard and why the PPE is required.
- Record the observation, including location, task, and persons involved.

## Short-term (next 7 days)

- Review whether PPE requirements are clearly posted and communicated.
- Check availability, sizing, and comfort of PPE in the area.
- Discuss trends in PPE noncompliance during toolbox talks.
- Verify that supervisors reinforce expectations consistently.

## Preventive (next 30 days)

- Analyze patterns (locations, tasks, contractors) where PPE noncompliance is common.
- Address root causes (e.g. discomfort, lack of training, poor supervision).
- Consider engineering or administrative controls that reduce reliance on PPE alone.
- Confirm that disciplinary/escalation pathways are clear but fair.
