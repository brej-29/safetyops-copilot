# Runbook: Falls and Slips

## Immediate actions (today)

- Stop work in the affected area and secure the scene.
- Provide medical attention to anyone injured; call emergency services if needed.
- Remove immediate hazards (wet floors, loose cables, unprotected edges).
- Record names of witnesses and take photos if appropriate.
- Notify the site supervisor and safety representative.

## Short-term (next 7 days)

- Conduct a brief root-cause review (conditions, behaviors, controls).
- Verify that housekeeping standards are clear and enforced.
- Check that guardrails, handrails, and fall protection systems are in place.
- Update local toolbox talks to address fall hazards observed.

## Preventive (next 30 days)

- Review incident trends for falls/slips/trips.
- Refresh training on working at height and safe access routes.
- Inspect high-risk areas (stairs, ladders, loading docks) on a regular schedule.
- Confirm that findings and corrective actions are logged in the safety system.
