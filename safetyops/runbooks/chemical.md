# Runbook: Chemical Spills and Exposures

## Immediate actions (today)

- Stop work and secure the area; prevent further releases if safe to do so.
- Isolate ignition sources and enhance ventilation if appropriate.
- Follow the Safety Data Sheet (SDS) for first aid and spill response.
- Use appropriate PPE before entering the spill area.
- Call emergency services if there is significant release, inhalation risk, or injury.
- Notify the site safety officer and environmental representative.

## Short-term (next 7 days)

- Confirm that SDS documents are accessible and up to date.
- Review storage and labeling of chemicals in the affected area.
- Check spill kits (availability, completeness, and training on use).
- Document the incident and any exposure evaluations.

## Preventive (next 30 days)

- Review chemical inventory and compatibility in storage.
- Update training on chemical handling, PPE, and emergency response.
- Improve engineering controls (bunding, ventilation, containment) if needed.
- Verify that waste disposal practices comply with regulations.
