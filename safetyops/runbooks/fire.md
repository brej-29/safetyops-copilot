# Runbook: Fire and Smoke

## Immediate actions (today)

- Raise the alarm; follow site evacuation procedures.
- Call emergency services if there is active fire or heavy smoke.
- Use fire extinguishers **only if trained and safe to do so**.
- Account for personnel at the assembly point.
- Do not re-enter the area until authorized by competent personnel.

## Short-term (next 7 days)

- Investigate ignition sources (equipment, wiring, hot work, smoking).
- Check housekeeping and storage of flammables in the affected area.
- Inspect fire detection and suppression systems for proper function.
- Review hot-work permits and controls if relevant.

## Preventive (next 30 days)

- Refresh training on fire response and extinguisher use.
- Verify that escape routes and exits are unobstructed.
- Review storage and handling of combustible/flammable materials.
- Ensure fire drills are planned and documented.
