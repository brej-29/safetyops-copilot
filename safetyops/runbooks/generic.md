# Runbook: General Safety Incident

## Immediate actions (today)

- Stop or control the unsafe activity or condition.
- Ensure injured persons receive appropriate medical attention.
- Make the area safe to prevent repeat incidents.
- Notify the responsible supervisor and safety representative.
- Preserve evidence (photos, equipment, witness names) where appropriate.

## Short-term (next 7 days)

- Conduct a brief root-cause review with those involved.
- Identify which controls failed or were missing (engineering, admin, PPE).
- Implement quick corrective actions and verify they are effective.
- Communicate lessons learned in toolbox talks or team briefings.

## Preventive (next 30 days)

- Review trends across similar incidents or near misses.
- Check that procedures, risk assessments, and training materials are current.
- Improve supervision and monitoring in higher-risk areas.
- Ensure all actions are tracked to completion in the safety management system.
